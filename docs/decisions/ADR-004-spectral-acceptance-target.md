# ADR-004: Spectral Acceptance Target Revision for Internal Dynamics

## Status

**Accepted.** This ADR implements **Deliverable (b)** of **T-FIX-10** (*Spectral acceptance resolution*) as specified in `docs/lab_phase/PRE_REAL_PERCEPTION_FIX_ROADMAP.md` §T-FIX-10. It is formally traceable to the scientific findings established in `docs/reviews/OSCILLATOR_COUPLING.md` (T-FIX-09) and supersedes the preliminary target specified in `docs/ROADMAP.md` §Task 3.7.

---

## Context

Task 3.7 (`tests/integration/test_spectrum.py::test_project_spectral_acceptance`) evaluates the power spectral density (PSD) characteristics of the 5-dimensional `MetaState` time series generated by the composed `Internal Dynamics` subsystem (`Drives`, `OscillatorBank`, `LorenzAttractor`, `MemoryStore`, coordinated by `MetaStateGenerator`).

The original project specification (`docs/ROADMAP.md:405`) specified:
```text
scientific target [-1.5, -0.5] (pink noise ~ 1/f)
```
across a Welch PSD linear regression fitting band of $[0.005, 2.5]\text{ Hz}$ under a deterministic 10,000-step simulation ($dt = 0.1\text{ s}$, duration $1{,}000\text{ s}$, warmup $1{,}000$ steps, zero events).

During pre-Phase-13 verification (`docs/reviews/PRE_PHASE_13_REVIEW.md` §2.2), this test consistently failed. All five drives exhibited steep spectral slopes of $\approx -6.1$ with near-perfect fit quality ($R^2 \approx 0.998$):
- `hunger`: slope $= -6.1176$ ($R^2 = 0.9982$)
- `fatigue`: slope $= -6.2234$ ($R^2 = 0.9951$)
- `curiosity`: slope $= -6.0928$ ($R^2 = 0.9989$)
- `aggression`: slope $= -6.0928$ ($R^2 = 0.9989$)
- `social`: slope $= -6.0844$ ($R^2 = 0.9991$)

Task **T-FIX-09** was commissioned to investigate the physical and mathematical basis of this discrepancy. Its report (`docs/reviews/OSCILLATOR_COUPLING.md`) established the following definitive findings:

1. **The System Is Bandlimited Below $0.02\text{ Hz}$:**
   The `OscillatorBank` comprises five canonical discrete sinusoids with frequencies:
   $$f \in \{5.55\times 10^{-5}, 1.85\times 10^{-4}, 8.33\times 10^{-4}, 3.33\times 10^{-3}, 1.67\times 10^{-2}\}\text{ Hz}$$
   The highest frequency is $1/60 \approx 0.0167\text{ Hz}$. Above $0.0167\text{ Hz}$, the oscillator bank contributes zero continuous spectral power.

2. **Timescale Dilation of Chaos:**
   `LorenzAttractor` is integrated at a fixed $h = 0.001$, while simulation steps advance with $dt = 0.1\text{ s}$. Over 1,000 seconds, the Lorenz attractor advances by only 10 dimensionless time units (~10–13 orbits total), compressing chaotic orbit dynamics below $0.01\text{ Hz}$. Above $0.02\text{ Hz}$, the RK4 sub-step trajectory is an ultra-smooth curve with negligible broadband energy.

3. **Origin of the $-6.1$ Slope:**
   In Welch's method (`scipy.signal.welch`) using a standard Hann window, the window Fourier transform is:
   $$W(f) = \frac{\sin(\pi f T)}{2\pi f (1 - f^2 T^2)} \sim O\left(\frac{1}{f^3}\right) \quad \text{for } f T \gg 1$$
   The resulting power spectral density sidelobe roll-off decays as:
   $$S_{\text{leakage}}(f) \propto |W(f)|^2 \sim O\left(\frac{1}{f^6}\right)$$
   In $\log_{10}(f)$ vs $\log_{10}(S)$ coordinates, the theoretical asymptotic slope of Hann window sidelobe leakage is **identically $-6.0$**.
   Because the unperturbed internal dynamics system produces zero physical power above $0.02\text{ Hz}$, the Welch PSD across the fitting band $[0.005, 2.5]\text{ Hz}$ is measuring the Hann window sidelobe leakage floor.

4. **Mathematical Impossibility of Achieving $[-1.5, -0.5]$ Under Existing Dynamics:**
   - Coupling the discrete 5-oscillator bank into `Drives` adds discrete delta peaks at $f \le 0.0167\text{ Hz}$, leaving the high-frequency slope at $-6.1$.
   - Even when Lorenz is matched 1:1 with simulation time (yielding an intrinsic Lorenz slope of $-1.03$), the leaky integrator of `Drives` ($H(f) \sim 1/f$, $|H(f)|^2 \sim 1/f^2$) steepens the slope by $-2.0$, resulting in a slope of $-3.10$.
   - A discrete multi-oscillator bank coupled to a continuous leaky integrator cannot produce a continuous $1/f$ pink noise power law across $0.02 - 2.5\text{ Hz}$ without continuous broadband stochastic driving.

---

## Decision

We formally revise the unperturbed spectral acceptance target in `docs/ROADMAP.md` §Task 3.7 and `tests/integration/test_spectrum.py`.

### 1. Revised Scientific Acceptance Criterion

For the unperturbed deterministic simulation ($N = 10{,}000$, warmup $= 1{,}000$, $dt = 0.1\text{ s}$, seed $= 42$, zero external events, fitting band $[0.005, 2.5]\text{ Hz}$):

- **Target Range:** Each drive log-log PSD slope must fall within:
  $$[-6.5, -5.5]$$
- **Fit Quality:** Linear regression $R^2 \ge 0.99$ for each drive dimension.
- **Finite & Non-zero Variance:** All drive signals must exhibit measurable variance ($\text{std} > 10^{-6}$) and finite slopes.

### 2. Scientific Rationale

1. **Truth in Telemetry & Physics:**
   The revised target directly reflects the physical reality of the deterministic subsystem. The slope of $-6.0 \pm 0.5$ proves that:
   - The unperturbed drives are smooth and bandlimited to sub-minute ultradian timescales ($f \le 0.0167\text{ Hz}$).
   - High-frequency numerical noise or instability is absent, falling strictly along the theoretical $-6.0$ Hann window sidelobe leakage bound.
   - The system is free of high-frequency jitter, unconstrained white noise, or runaway divergence.

2. **Compliance with Governance Rules:**
   - `docs/local_validation_docs/LOCAL_VALIDATION_ROADMAP.md` §6.2-6.3 strictly prohibits tuning seeds, bands, or dynamics to mask results.
   - `docs/lab_phase/PRE_REAL_PERCEPTION_FIX_ROADMAP.md` explicitly authorizes Deliverable (b): a documented target revision via an ADR explaining why $[-1.5, -0.5]$ is not the right acceptance criterion for the implemented dynamics.
   - No `skip`, `xfail`, or retry markers are introduced; the assertion verifies the true mathematical characteristics of the implemented model.

3. **Phase 13 Roadmapping:**
   If the research team desires true broadband $1/f$ pink noise modulation in the reflex frequency band ($0.1 - 2.5\text{ Hz}$) during live gameplay, this should be addressed as an intentional Phase 13 feature (e.g. via stochastic fractional Ornstein-Uhlenbeck noise or timescale-matched Lorenz coupling). It does not gate MOCK_MODE or LAB_MODE actuation.
