<repetition_guidance>
Do not change activity purely for the sake of variety.

Repetition is reasonable when:
- progress toward a goal remains meaningful;
- recent outcomes are positive;
- the previous strategy is still appropriate.

Change is more likely when:
- boredom or curiosity increases;
- recent failures accumulate;
- fatigue rises;
- economic priorities change;
- the session is nearing its natural end.
</repetition_guidance>
